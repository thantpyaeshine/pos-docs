# Features of the Point-of-Sale (POS) System

## User and Access Management

- Authenticate users through multiple supported login methods.
- Allow administrators to create and manage roles with specific permissions.
- Allow administrators to assign roles and permissions to employees.
- Restrict system features and data according to each user's role and permissions.
- User profile management, including updating personal information and changing passwords.
- Allow users to securely log out and enforce session timeouts for inactive users.
- Provide password recovery options for users who forget their passwords.
- Users account activation and deactivation.

## Sales Transactions and Receipts

- Process sales by applying discounts and taxes and calculating totals.
- Accept multiple payment methods for a transaction, including split payments, and record cash change.
- Record each transaction and the user who processed it for reporting and auditing.
- Allow authorized users to void or cancel transactions and process returns or exchanges.
- Generate receipts that can include delivery information, delivery fees, and customized items.
- Allow authorized users to customize receipt content and layout.

## Inventory Management

- Add, update, remove, categorize, and search inventory items.
- Track stock levels and notify users when items are low in stock.
- Reflect sales, returns, and inventory adjustments across connected devices.
- Generate inventory reports for analysis and auditing.

## Customer Management and Credit

- Add, update, remove, search, and categorize customer records.
- Track customer purchase history and generate customer reports.
- Support credit sales with defined customer credit limits.
- Notify users about credit due dates and outstanding balances.

## Supplier Management

- Add, update, remove, search, and categorize supplier records.
- Track supplier order history and generate supplier reports.

## Search and Filtering

- Search inventory items, customers, suppliers, sales, and employees using relevant criteria such as name, category, and ID.
- Filter results by attributes such as date, status, and location, and sort by relevant fields such as name, date, and quantity.
- Provide search suggestions and autocomplete.
- Allow users to save and reuse common search queries.

## Reporting, Analytics, and Audit

- Generate sales reports, including top sellers and slow movers.
- Provide reports for inventory, customers, and suppliers.
- Present dashboards with daily insights into sales, inventory, customer, and employee data.
- Keep logs of changes to sales, inventory, customer, employee, and supplier data, as well as security-related events.

## Notifications, Connectivity, and Recovery

- Notify users about important events, including low stock, credit due dates, and outstanding balances.
- Support offline operation during temporary connectivity loss and notify users when connectivity is lost or restored.
- Perform regular data backups and support recovery after failures.

## Responsive Experience and Usability

- Provide a responsive interface for mobile-targeted use and different screen sizes.
- Provide clear error messages, contextual help, and tooltips.
- Minimize steps for common tasks such as processing sales, finding items, and updating inventory.
