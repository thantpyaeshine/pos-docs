# Scope of the Point-of-Sale (POS) System

## In Scope

- Sales processing and management
- Inventory management, including stock tracking and updates
- Customer management, including purchase history and categorization
- Supplier management, including order tracking and categorization
- Reporting and analytics for sales, inventory, customers, and suppliers
- User authentication and role-based access control
- Notifications and alerts for important events and actions
- Data backup and recovery mechanisms
- Mobile targeted responsive design

## Out of Scope

- Integration with external accounting systems
- Advanced AI-based analytics and forecasting
- Hardware management, such as POS terminals and barcode scanners
- Integration with third-party payment gateways
